# Phone, mockups, shipping and Remote

## Mockups

**Work → Mockups** shows the mockups your designer bee made. Use **Find a mockup** to filter them.

## Phone

**Work → Phone** is for mobile apps made with Expo. See the app:

- in a phone on the screen,
- on your own phone, with a QR code,
- or in the iPhone simulator.

If an install was stopped, the Phone tab repairs it.

## Ship

**Work → Ship** puts your project on the internet.

- Deploy to **Render**.
- Connect a domain through **Cloudflare**. Domains with accents, like café.com, work too.
- Your keys for Render and Cloudflare stay in the macOS Keychain.
- **Disconnect** removes the Render and Cloudflare servers from every project.

## Notifications on your Mac

ULU tells you when a task is done (with its cost and time), when the team needs you, when a team is built, and when Kukulu answers. They show only when ULU is not in front. Turn them on or off in **Help → Notifications**.

## ULU Remote (paid)

Watch the hive from your phone, anywhere:

- see what the team is doing and what it costs,
- answer its questions and approve a design,
- stop a task,
- get notifications on your phone.

Nothing to install from a store.

1. Press **Remote** in the top bar.
2. Pair your phone with the QR code.
3. On iPhone, add ULU Remote to your Home Screen to get notifications.

Everything between your Mac and your phone is encrypted end to end. ULU Remote needs a paid project key. Your first project stays free.
