# Costs and budget

ULU itself does not charge you for Claude. ULU uses Claude Code on your Mac, so every step that uses Claude spends your Claude usage: a design answer, a build, a task, a report or an answer in Ask.

## What ULU shows

- The **coin** at the top shows what the open project has cost.
- The list of projects shows the cost of each project.
- The Hive shows the cost of each run, and of each bee in it.
- The numbers on top of the Hive add up all runs: cost of all runs, average for a run, time, and which bee costs the most.

The cost of a design answer, a build and a finished task is the number Claude Code reports. The cost of each bee is an estimate from the tokens it read and wrote, at Anthropic's list prices. It can differ a little.

## Set a budget

Click the coin at the top. When the project has no budget yet, it says **Set a budget**. The window **Prices and budget** opens.

1. Type how much the project may cost.
2. Press **Save the budget**.

The bar at the top then shows how much of the budget is spent. ULU tells you at 80% and when the budget is used up. **The budget does not stop the team.** It is a warning, so you stay in control.

The same window shows what was spent on tasks, the last build, design, brief and Ask.

## Prices

In the same window, **Prices** lists the price of a million tokens for each model. They start at Anthropic's list prices. Change them if you pay another price. **Back to the list prices** undoes your changes.

## Spend less

- The **Model** menu at the top sets the model for the design, the build and El Capitan. Each bee uses the model its design gives it.
- A cheaper model for simple jobs, such as running tests, lowers the cost. Change it in **Design**.
- Use **Only** one bee for small jobs, instead of the whole team.
- **Search only** in Ask is free.

## Your license

- One project is free.
- More projects need a paid key. Press **Buy more projects** in the **License** window.
- ULU Remote needs a paid key.
- A project takes its place on your key when you first start work in it: a design message, a build, a task or a new report. It keeps that place, also when you rename or move it.
- Opening ULU, the list of projects and reading saved reports need no key.
- Without the internet, ULU goes on working for 14 days after the last check.
- A beta key works offline, with no account.
